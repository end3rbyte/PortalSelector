# PortalSelector

PortalSelector gives each player personal portal names and destinations. Shared tags and connections stay unchanged. Only the client needs the mod.

## Controls

- **E:** Rename a portal for your character. Names can be up to 50 characters and must be unique within the world.
- **Shift + E:** Choose a confirmed destination, listed alphabetically by personal name and biome. Press **OK** to save or **Cancel** to leave it unchanged.

The hover text shows your portal name, destination, connection status, and read-only shared tag. Portals enter your list when encountered. Known destinations are checked in the background, one every 250 ms. Destroyed or unreachable portals are removed.

New personal names start from shared tags; paired portals get `_1` and `_2` suffixes. An existing shared connection is copied as the initial personal destination. Names, destinations, and known portals are stored with your character for each world and written on Valheim's next normal save.

The classic wooden portal costs **40 fine wood, 20 greydwarf eyes, and 4 surtling cores**. Install PortalSelector and BepInExPack Valheim on your client.
